Keepin Inc. ((주)키핀, the "Company") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act (개인정보 보호법) and other applicable laws, and establishes and discloses this Privacy Policy as follows to protect the personal information of users of Klovin (the "Service").

Article 1 Purposes of Processing Personal Information

The Company processes personal information for the following purposes, and will obtain prior consent if a purpose changes.

  1. Sign-up and account management — identifying members, keeping users logged in, verifying that users are 19 or older, preventing misuse, delivering notices, and responding to inquiries
  2. Profiles and meeting people — displaying profiles, discovery and recommendations (showing people who match the user's interests and the "people I want to meet" criteria the user sets), and following
  3. Posts, stories and comments — publishing and displaying photos and text, applying visibility and comment settings, likes and comments
  4. Friends and the space for two — friend requests (a short message or a short voice message), friendships, relationship status ("us"), scheduled letters, anniversary cards, decorations and gifts
  5. Translated chat — sending text, photos and translated voice messages, automatic translation into the recipient's language, read receipts, and warnings about suspected scam messages
  6. Voice and video calls with real-time translated captions — connecting and relaying calls, real-time transcription and translated captions of call audio, and playing translated speech in the voice the user has registered
  7. AI friends — generating chat replies, posts and photos for AI friends (virtual characters) created by the Service
  8. Hearts and paid services — granting, using and managing the expiry of Hearts, ad rewards, invitation benefits, and payments and refunds (where payment features are offered)
  9. Safety — handling reports and blocks, screenshot alerts, and usage restrictions
  10. Sending notifications — push notifications for messages, calls, friend requests, comments and more
  11. Service improvement and stable operation — identifying errors and improving quality

Article 3 Processing of Information When Using Translation and Artificial Intelligence (AI) Features

Translation is the core feature of the Service. When the following features are used, the necessary information is sent to and processed by the relevant providers (Articles 6 and 7).

  1. Text translation — Chat messages, post text, comments, bios, friend request messages, letters, and short messages on gifts and cards are translated by Google Gemini into the language of the recipient (or viewer). For chat messages, a few of the immediately preceding messages are also sent as context so the meaning can be translated accurately. Translations are stored on the server so that the same content is not translated again.
  2. Scam-suspicion warnings — When a chat message is translated, it is also checked for signs of a possible scam, such as requests for money transfers, attempts to move the conversation to an external messenger, or external links, and a warning is shown only to the recipient.
  3. Translated voice messages and Super request voice messages — Recordings are transcribed and translated by Google Gemini, and for translated voice messages Soniox produces speech reading the translation in the voice the sender has registered.
  4. Real-time call captions — If captions are on during a call, the user's call audio is sent to Soniox in real time for transcription and translation. When "Push to talk" is used, speech reading the translation in the voice the user has registered is delivered to the other person. Captions are only displayed on screen and are not stored on the Company's servers.
  5. Registering my voice — A voice model is created at Soniox from the voice the user records. It is deleted via [Release] in Settings or by deleting the account. The registered voice is used only for translated speech sent by the user.
  6. AI friends — The Service has AI friends (virtual characters marked "AI") created by the Company. Messages and photos sent to an AI friend, transcriptions of voice messages, and recent conversation are sent to Google Gemini to generate replies. For natural conversation, a summary (memory) of the conversation with the AI friend and a closeness score are stored on the server. Photos sent by AI friends are AI-generated images, not real people. AI friends can be hidden in Settings.

Information sent to AI providers is processed according to each provider's policies, and the Company does not use it to train the Company's AI models. Translations and AI responses may be inaccurate.

Article 4 Information Visible to Other Users

  • To all users — profile photos, name, username, age, country, city (if the user chose to make it public), language spoken, interests, bio, public posts and their comments, stories
  • To friends only — friends-only posts, chats and calls, the "us" screen (letters, anniversaries, decorations, gifts exchanged), translated speech created with my voice
  • To the user only — date of birth, "people I want to meet" criteria, follower and like counts, Hearts, invitation code, settings
  • Read receipts can be turned off in Settings. Relationship status ("us") changes only when both people choose it, and what I choose is not shown to the other person.

Article 8 Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

The Company does not provide users' personal information to third parties beyond the purposes in Article 1. Exceptions are when the user has given prior consent, when there are special provisions in law, or when an investigative agency requests it according to the procedures and methods prescribed by law. Information that users post or send themselves being visible to other users, as described in Article 4, is a basic function of the Service.

Article 9 Advertising

The Service displays ads in the home feed through Google AdMob and shows rewarded ads when the user wants them (watching an ad to the end lets the user send one friend request for free). AdMob may process the phone's advertising identifier and device information to display ads and measure their effectiveness. Users can reset the advertising identifier or turn off personalized ads in their phone's settings. The Company does not use users' posts or chat content for advertising.

Article 10 Users' Rights and Obligations and How to Exercise Them

Users may at any time request access to, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of their personal information, or withdraw consent, and most of this can be done directly in the app.

  • Editing your profile — Profile tab → Edit profile
  • Deleting posts, comments and stories — delete from each item's menu
  • Visibility — set visibility and comment settings for each post; change city visibility, read receipts and AI friend visibility in Settings
  • Releasing my voice — Settings → My voice → Remove
  • Blocking and reporting — from the menu on a profile, post, comment or message
  • Deleting your account — Profile tab → Settings → Delete account. Your account and profile, posts, comments, likes and stories, chats and the space for two, friend and follow relationships, my voice, and Heart history are deleted and cannot be recovered. Information that must be retained by law is handled as described in Article 5. Details: Account and Data Deletion

If you make a request by email or other means, please submit it to the contact in Article 14, and we will act without delay after verifying your identity. If you make the request through an agent, a power of attorney must be submitted.

Article 11 Personal Information of Users Under 19

The Service does not allow people under 19 to sign up (checked by date of birth at sign-up) and does not knowingly collect personal information of people under 19. Accounts suspected of belonging to someone under 19 can be reported, and if we become aware of this, we will delete the account and its information without delay.

Article 13 Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information

  • Encryption in transit — All communication between the app and servers, and between servers and contractors, is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Access control — Row-level access rules are applied to every table in the database, so users can read only their own information and information they have permission to see. Chats and calls are possible only between friends.
  • Separate storage of secret keys — Authentication keys for translation, AI and call providers are not included in the app and are kept only on the server.
  • Screenshot prevention — On Android, screenshots and screen recording are blocked on the chat, space for two, enlarged photo view and story screens; on iPhone, the other person is notified when a chat is captured.
  • Safety features — scam-suspicion warnings, reporting and blocking, and restricting chats and calls with people who are not friends
  • Least privilege — Access to systems that process personal information is limited to the minimum number of people.
