Child Safety Standards

Developer: Keepin Inc. · App: Klovin · Effective 2026-10-22

Klovin has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page explains the standards Klovin follows to protect children and how to report concerns.

1. Adults only (19+)

  • Only people aged 19 or older can sign up for Klovin. Age is checked with the date of birth at sign-up.
  • Accounts suspected of belonging to a minor can be reported, and once confirmed, the account and its data are deleted without delay.

2. Prohibited conduct

The following are prohibited under our Terms of Service. When found, the content is removed and the account is permanently banned without warning.

  • Uploading, sending, requesting, or sharing links to child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Any content that sexualizes children or depicts them in a sexual way
  • Grooming (building trust with a child in order to exploit them), sexual exploitation, or sextortion
  • Arranging or advertising the sexual exploitation or trafficking of children
  • Minors signing up by lying about their age, or attempts to meet minors

3. Reporting in the app

  • Tap ⋮ › Report on any post, comment, chat message, or profile and choose "Suspected minor" or another relevant reason.
  • You can also block the person right away. Once blocked, you can no longer see each other's profile, posts, or chats.
  • "Suspected minor" reports are a zero-tolerance category and are reviewed by our moderators with priority.
  • If you cannot use the app or the matter is urgent, email help@keepin.co.kr.

4. How we act

  • Moderators review each report. When CSAE or CSAM is confirmed, the content is removed and the account is permanently banned. Banned accounts cannot sign in and are hidden from other users.
  • When CSAM is confirmed, we preserve the relevant records and report it to law enforcement and the relevant authorities as required by law (such as the Korean National Police Agency and the Korea Communications Standards Commission, and for users abroad the authorities of that country or NCMEC).
  • The person who reported is notified in the app that the report has been handled.

5. Compliance

Klovin complies with applicable child safety laws, including the Korean Act on the Protection of Children and Youth against Sex Offenses and the Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection, as well as Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.
