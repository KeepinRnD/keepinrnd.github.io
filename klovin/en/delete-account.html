Account and Data Deletion

Developer: Keepin Inc. ((주)키핀) · App: Klovin

This is a translation of the Korean original. If the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

You can delete your Klovin account and related data directly in the app. If you cannot use the app, you can also request deletion by email.

Delete directly in the app

  1. Open the Klovin app and log in.
  2. On the Profile tab at the bottom, tap Settings (the gear icon) in the top right.
  3. In the Account section at the very bottom, tap Delete account and confirm by following the instructions.

When you delete your account, your account and data are deleted immediately, and deleted information cannot be recovered.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, please send a deletion request to the address below, including the account you used to sign up (Google, Kakao or Apple) and your username. We will process it after verifying your identity.

Request emailhelp@keepin.co.kr

Processing timeProcessed without delay after the request is confirmed (except for items that must be retained by law)

Data that is deleted

  • Login account and member identification information
  • Profile (photos, name, username, gender, date of birth, country, city, language spoken, interests, bio), "people I want to meet" criteria, settings
  • Posts and stories (photos and text), comments and replies, likes
  • Follow and friend relationships, friend requests sent and received (including short voice messages)
  • Chats (messages, photos, voice messages, translations) and the space for two (letters, anniversary cards, decorations, gifts) — because these are shared by two people, they also disappear from the other person's screen
  • My voice (voice model for translated speech)
  • Conversation summary and closeness with AI friends
  • Heart balance and history, ad reward and invitation records
  • Call records, report and block lists, push notification tokens

Data retained for a set period

Under applicable laws, the following records are kept for the specified periods and then destroyed.

  • Records of contracts or withdrawal of subscription: 5 years
  • Records of payment and supply of goods, etc.: 5 years
  • Records of consumer complaints or dispute handling: 3 years
  • Service access logs: 3 months

If you have a subscription, payments stop only when you cancel it in Google Play or App Store subscription management, separately from deleting your account. Remaining Hearts are forfeited when you delete your account. For details, see the Privacy Policy.
